# Yahya_Restaurant Restaurant - Policy & General Information

> **Document Type:** Restaurant Policy, Branch Information, Services, Operating Hours, Discounts, and Customer Information
> **Last Information Update:** September 2026
> **Currency:** Egyptian Pound (EGP)

## 1. Restaurant Overview

Yahya_Restaurant is a casual restaurant serving Egyptian food, grilled meals, fast food, sandwiches, pizza, pasta, salads, desserts, and hot and cold drinks.

### Main Services
- Dine-in service.
- Takeaway and pickup.
- Home delivery.
- Online and phone ordering.
- Table reservations.
- Family meals and group orders.
- Corporate and office meal orders.
- Birthday and small-event food arrangements.
- Catering for pre-arranged events.

## 3. Opening and Closing Policy

- Branches operate every day of the week unless a temporary closure is announced.
- Delivery orders may stop 30 minutes before the listed branch closing time.
- Kitchen last-order time is usually 30 minutes before closing.
- During public holidays and special events, operating hours may change.
- Customers should check the branch-specific phone number for temporary schedule changes.

## 4. Ordering Policy

### Dine-In
- Customers may order directly from their table.
- Table reservations are recommended for groups of 6 or more.
- Large groups may be asked to confirm the reservation with a phone number.

### Takeaway
- Customers can place orders by phone or in person.
- Pickup orders are prepared after order confirmation.
- Customers should provide the order number when collecting a prepaid order.

### Delivery
- Delivery availability depends on the delivery area of each branch.
- Estimated delivery time is normally 30-60 minutes.
- Delivery times may be longer during peak hours, weekends, holidays, traffic, or bad weather.
- The delivery fee depends on distance and is confirmed before the order is finalized.
- A minimum order value of 150 EGP applies to delivery orders before delivery fees.

## 5. Payment Methods

Yahya_Restaurant accepts:
- Cash in Egyptian Pounds.
- Visa and Mastercard at participating branches.
- Mobile wallet payments at participating branches.
- Online card payment for supported delivery orders.

Payment method availability may vary by branch.

## 6. Discounts and Promotions

### Student Discount
- **Discount:** 10% off dine-in food items.
- **Eligibility:** Valid student ID is required.
- **Limit:** One student discount per customer per visit.
- **Exclusions:** Beverages, existing promotional bundles, and catering orders.

### Family Tuesday Offer
- **Offer:** 15% off selected family meals every Tuesday.
- **Eligible Items:** Family Chicken Platter and selected family meal combos.
- **Availability:** Participating branches only.
- **Cannot be combined with:** Other percentage discounts.

### Loyalty Program
- Customers earn **1 point for every 10 EGP** spent on eligible food and beverage purchases.
- **100 points = 50 EGP** loyalty credit.
- Points are added after payment is completed.
- Loyalty points cannot be exchanged for cash.
- Loyalty credits cannot be combined with certain promotional offers.

### Promotional Pricing
- Limited-time promotions may have different prices from the standard menu.
- The valid price shown at order confirmation is the price charged for the order.
- Promotions may have branch-specific availability.

## 7. Cancellation and Modification Policy

- Orders can be modified or cancelled before preparation begins.
- Once an order enters preparation, cancellation may not be available.
- For delivery orders, customers should contact the branch as soon as possible to request a cancellation.
- Refunds for prepaid cancelled orders are handled according to the payment method and processing rules.

## 8. Returns, Refunds, and Complaints

### Wrong or Missing Item
- Customers should contact the branch within 30 minutes of receiving the order.
- The restaurant may request the order number and a description of the problem.
- Missing or incorrect items may be replaced or refunded after verification.

### Food Quality Complaint
- Customers should report food-quality concerns as soon as possible.
- Complaints should include the order number, branch, and purchase time when available.
- The restaurant reviews complaints individually.

### Refunds
- Refund method depends on the original payment method.
- Cash refunds may be processed by the branch after verification.
- Card and wallet refunds may require additional processing time.

## 9. Allergies and Dietary Information

- Customers must inform staff about food allergies before ordering.
- Yahya_Restaurant prepares food in a shared kitchen environment.
- The restaurant cannot guarantee that an item is completely free from allergens because ingredients may share preparation areas.
- Common allergens may include dairy, eggs, gluten, nuts, soy, and seafood.
- Customers following a special diet should confirm ingredients with restaurant staff before ordering.

## 10. Delivery Policy

### Standard Delivery
- Typical delivery time: 30-60 minutes.
- Peak-period delivery time may exceed 60 minutes.
- Delivery fees are calculated by area and distance.
- Customers must provide a clear address and working phone number.

### Order Acceptance
- An order is considered confirmed after the branch accepts it.
- If an item becomes unavailable, the branch may contact the customer with an alternative or refund option.

### Contactless Delivery
- Contactless delivery is available upon request.
- Customers may request that the order be left at the door or another agreed location.

## 11. Reservations Policy

- Reservations can be requested by phone.
- Reservations are held for up to 15 minutes after the scheduled arrival time.
- Groups of 6 or more should reserve in advance.
- During peak periods, the restaurant may limit reservation duration to 90 minutes.
- Reservations do not guarantee availability of specific menu items.

## 12. Events and Catering

- Yahya_Restaurant provides catering for meetings, birthdays, office events, and small private gatherings.
- Catering orders should normally be placed at least 48 hours in advance.
- Custom menus are available depending on budget, guest count, dietary requirements, and menu availability.
- Catering prices are quoted separately from standard menu prices.
- Large catering orders may require a deposit.

## 13. Kids and Family Services

- Kids meals are available during normal branch operating hours.
- High chairs are available subject to branch capacity.
- Family meal bundles are designed for groups and may have different portions from individual menu items.

## 14. Seating and Restaurant Rules

- Smoking is not permitted inside the restaurant.
- Customers should keep walkways and emergency exits clear.
- Outside food and beverages are generally not permitted, except for approved medical or infant-related needs.
- The restaurant may refuse service in cases of abusive, threatening, or unsafe behavior.
- Customers are responsible for personal belongings.

## 15. Service Charge and Taxes

- Menu prices are displayed in Egyptian Pounds.
- Any applicable service charge will be shown on the bill.
- Delivery fees are separate from menu prices.
- Promotional discounts may apply only to eligible items.

## 16. Price and Menu Availability Policy

- Menu prices may change without prior notice.
- The price confirmed at the time of ordering is the applicable price for that order.
- Some items may be unavailable temporarily because of ingredient supply or branch inventory.
- Branches may have limited-time items that are not available at all locations.
